Understand the category before discussing the product.

Exosomes are small extracellular vesicles released by cells and studied for their role in intercellular communication. Research interest in exosomes does not establish the safety, effectiveness, approval, or suitability of a commercial product. Source material, isolation and characterisation methods, composition, intended use, quality controls, and regulatory classification must be evaluated for the exact offering.

This page provides general product and educational information. Product availability, classification, indication, administration requirements, registration status, and suitability vary. Information does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional or approved product documentation.
