Know your MSCs before you order.

For doctors and clinics evaluating mesenchymal stem cell products. Review what Passage 2 (P2) means and request the specifications and documents your team needs.

Request Product Sheet Build a document request

What are MSCs?

mesenchymal stem cells are cells that can be isolated from tissues such as the umbilical cord and grown in a laboratory.

How MSCs work

  1. Expansion in culture

    MSCs can divide under controlled laboratory conditions. As cells are transferred to new culture vessels, the manufacturer tracks each passage.

  2. Differentiation in the lab

    Under specific laboratory conditions, MSCs can form bone, cartilage and fat cell lineages. This property helps characterise the cells in the lab.

  3. Cell signalling

    MSCs release molecules that can influence nearby cells. Researchers study how these signals affect immune responses and the tissue environment.

Potential benefits under study

These examples describe MSC research. Clinical benefit depends on the specific preparation, intended use and supporting evidence.

01 / 09

  1. Inflammation

    Studies examine whether MSC signalling can change inflammatory responses and whether those changes improve clinical outcomes.

  2. Tissue repair

    Researchers investigate how MSCs interact with injured tissue and whether that can improve healing or function.

  3. Joint symptoms and function

    Some studies assess outcomes such as pain and function in people with knee osteoarthritis. Those outcomes require evidence for the specific preparation and use.

  4. Chronic conditions

    Researchers are studying MSCs in long-term conditions where inflammation plays a part. Results vary by condition and preparation, and most uses remain investigational.

  5. Ageing-related frailty

    Early trials have studied MSCs in older adults with frailty, measuring physical function and inflammation markers. Larger trials are needed.

  6. Exercise capacity

    Some trials record walking distance or exercise capacity as an outcome, for example in heart and lung conditions. Results are mixed.

  7. Skin

    Studies examine MSC signalling in skin ageing and wound healing. Most of this work is laboratory or early clinical research.

  8. Men's health

    Researchers are exploring MSCs in some urological conditions. Evidence is limited to small, early studies.

  9. Recovery and wellbeing

    Some studies record fatigue, sleep and quality of life as secondary outcomes. These are early findings, not established benefits.

What is Passage 2?

A passage is a step in which cells are transferred to a new culture vessel for further growth. Passage 2 (P2) describes a stage of expansion under the manufacturer’s counting method.

  • Confirm how the manufacturer counts passages and records the passage for each lot.
  • Passage number alone cannot establish purity, potency, safety or clinical benefit. Review it alongside the lot release results.

Common questions.

What does Passage 2 mean?

It identifies a stage in the manufacturer’s cell expansion process. Ask how the manufacturer defines and documents it for the product.

What documents should our clinic request?

Start with the current product information, Malaysian regulatory documentation, release specifications, a sample lot certificate and handling instructions.

Can our clinic order immediately?

Begin with a documentation request. Any supply arrangement is subject to verification of the exact product, clinic eligibility and applicable Malaysian requirements.
