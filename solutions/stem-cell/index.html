Early passage stem cells.

Umbilical cord mesenchymal stem cells for clinics and practitioners. Supplied at Passage 2 from cGMP certified partner laboratories, delivered across Malaysia with full documentation.

Request product sheet For patients

The body’s mother cell.

Mesenchymal stem cells can become nerve, bone and skin. Ours come from umbilical cord jelly , are immune privileged and grow in cGMP labs .

Three functions, one cell.

  1. Self renewal

    MSCs replicate through many cycles of cell division while keeping their undifferentiated state.

  2. Differentiation

    MSCs are multipotent. They can grow and develop into several different specialised cell types.

  3. Homing effect

    MSCs migrate toward sites of injury and inflammation, where they support the body’s own repair processes.

Why Passage 2 matters.

A passage is one round of growing stem cells in culture. The market sells cells anywhere from Passage 2 to Passage 7. Later passages are cheaper to produce, but each round lets more cells drift into differentiated, less potent forms.

99.8% pure stem cells at Passage 2, 0.2% differentiated

90.0% pure stem cells at Passage 3, 10% differentiated

  • Early passage cells keep stronger proliferation and multipotency.
  • Late passage cells show more signs of ageing and inflammatory signalling.
  • Early passage cells hold up better against oxidative stress.

Common questions.

Where do the stem cells come from?

They are mesenchymal stem cells derived from Wharton’s jelly of donated umbilical cords, processed in cGMP certified laboratories.

What does Passage 2 mean?

It means the cells have been through only two rounds of culture. Fewer passages keep a higher share of pure, undifferentiated stem cells.

Will the patient’s body reject the cells?

MSCs are considered immune privileged, so they rarely trigger an immune reaction. The treating doctor assesses suitability for each patient.

Who can order?

We supply registered clinics, doctors and healthcare institutions. Patients are referred to a partner clinic.
