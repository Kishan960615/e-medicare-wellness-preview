Understand the category before discussing the product.

Natural killer cells are white blood cells that form part of the innate immune system. NK-cell products belong to a specialised cell-therapy context and must not be presented as general immunity boosters. Product selection, cell configuration, dose, administration, eligibility, monitoring, manufacturing controls, and regulatory pathway require qualified professional assessment and product-specific documentation.

This page provides general product and educational information. Product availability, classification, indication, administration requirements, registration status, and suitability vary. Information does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional or approved product documentation.
