Scope of this notice

This notice applies to information submitted through the E Medicare and Wellness website, including enquiry forms and direct contact links. The final company identity, effective date, service-provider details, and privacy contact must be confirmed before publication.

Information we may collect

We may collect your name, organisation, role, email address, telephone number, country, product category, enquiry type, message, and the communication preferences or consent you provide. Basic technical and usage information may also be collected if privacy-conscious website analytics are enabled.

Please do not submit sensitive health information

This website is not a patient portal or clinical service. Do not submit medical records, prescriptions, identification documents, laboratory results, photographs of medical conditions, or detailed personal health histories. If such information is received unintentionally, handling and deletion will be considered according to applicable requirements.

How information may be used

Submitted information may be used to respond to your enquiry, understand the relevant product or partnership pathway, route the request to an appropriate representative, maintain business correspondence, protect the website from misuse, and meet applicable legal or record-keeping obligations.

Service providers and transfers

Form, email, hosting, security, and analytics providers may process limited information on behalf of the company. The providers, processing locations, safeguards, and retention arrangements must be confirmed before launch, particularly where information may be processed outside Malaysia.

Retention and security

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, business relationship, security, or applicable obligations. Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards should be used, although no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact the company to ask about information associated with your enquiry or request correction or deletion, subject to identity verification, applicable law, legitimate business needs, and record-keeping requirements.

Contact and updates

The final privacy contact, effective date, review date, and process for announcing material changes must be supplied before publication.
